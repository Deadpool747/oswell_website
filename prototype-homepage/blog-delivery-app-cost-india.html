Cost of Building a Delivery App in India (2026 Guide)

Delivery app pricing depends on scope, timeline, and reliability requirements. Start with a practical MVP and expand using real user feedback.

Core cost drivers

  • Number of apps: customer, rider, and admin panel.
  • Real-time tracking and mapping complexity.
  • Payment gateway and order settlement flows.
  • Notification infrastructure and support tooling.

Typical build phases

Phase 1 - MVP: essential ordering, assignment, and status tracking.

Phase 2 - Optimization: dispatch logic, analytics, and customer engagement.

Phase 3 - Scale: multi-city support, ops automation, and SLA reporting.

Budget planning range

Early-stage MVPs usually start in the mid five-figure INR range, while production-grade multi-role systems require larger investment based on scale and compliance requirements.
